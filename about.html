OUR STORY

Twenty-five years of watching backs recover.

SpineXpert began with a simple conviction held by our founding physiotherapists: most spine problems do not need surgery — they need someone who understands the spine mechanically and treats it that way.

Over more than two decades, that conviction became a method. Advance biomechanical spinal adjustment — a chiropractic-style precision treatment — and world-class osteopathy form the hands-on core. The ROBOSPINE decompression machine, which works wonders for disc-bulge patients, forms the technological one. Assessment-first thinking ties them together.

Today the clinic serves Habsiguda, Vikrampuri and the wider Secunderabad-Hyderabad community, open seven days a week so that care never has to wait for the weekend to end.

OUR APPROACH

The Three Pillars of Spine Care

01

Assess Precisely

Posture, gait, nerve tension and joint-by-joint movement — a mechanical diagnosis that explains your pain in plain words before anything is prescribed.

02

Treat Non-Surgically First

ROBOSPINE decompression, biomechanical spinal adjustment and osteopathy resolve the great majority of spine problems — surgery stays a last resort, not a default.

03

Rebuild to Last

Strength, core stability and habit retraining turn short-term relief into a spine you can trust at work, at the gym and at rest.
