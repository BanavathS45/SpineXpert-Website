FAQS

Questions, answered honestly.

The things patients most often want to know before their first visit — timings, safety, sessions and what to expect.

Do I need a doctor's referral to visit? ▾
No, you do not need a prior physician or orthopedic referral to visit SpineXpert. You can book an appointment directly with us. If you have previous MRI scans, X-rays, or medical reports, bringing them along is helpful during your initial assessment.
What happens at the first consultation? ▾
Your first visit begins with a thorough mechanical evaluation: taking a detailed history of your symptoms, posture assessment, spinal range of motion checks, orthopedic tests, and neurological screenings (reflexes and nerve tension). We explain the findings clearly, discuss goals, and initiate your personalized treatment plan right away.
Is spinal decompression treatment painful? ▾
Not at all. In fact, most patients find ROBOSPINE decompression deeply relaxing and soothing. The computer-controlled distraction gently relieves pressure inside damaged discs without sudden or aggressive pulling. Many patients even rest or doze off during the session.
Can a slip disc heal without surgery? ▾
Yes, studies show that over 90% of disc bulges and herniations can heal effectively without surgical intervention. By reducing intradiscal pressure through precision decompression, calming local inflammation, and retraining core stability muscles, the disc can resorb and symptoms resolve naturally.
How many sessions will I need? ▾
The number of sessions depends on whether your condition is acute (recent) or chronic (persisting for months/years), as well as the extent of disc or nerve involvement. Most patients experience notable pain relief within 3 to 6 sessions, while full stabilization protocols typically span 10 to 18 sessions.

How does Osteopathy and Chiropractic care help migraines and headaches? ▾
A large percentage of migraines and chronic headaches originate in the upper cervical spine (atlas and axis vertebrae) and surrounding myofascial tissues. Restricting blood flow and irritating the greater occipital nerve causes severe throbbing pain. Our osteopathic cranial therapy and gentle chiropractic spinal manipulation restore cervical articulation, relieve pressure on the brainstem and cranial dura, and facilitate proper venous drainage—offering profound relief without daily reliance on analgesics.
What is Visceral Osteopathy and how can it treat gut issues? ▾
Visceral Osteopathy explores the functional relationship between your internal organs (viscera) and the musculoskeletal system. Your digestive organs (stomach, liver, intestines) are tethered by connective tissue ligaments to the diaphragm, ribs, and lower spine. Spasms, surgical adhesions, poor posture, or chronic stress restrict natural organ motility, leading to acid reflux, bloating, constipation, and abdominal discomfort. By applying gentle manual pressure to visceral fascial attachments and balancing thoracic sympathetic nerves, our therapists restore normal gut rhythm and ease visceral tension.
Will I get exercises to do at home? ▾
Absolutely. Active rehabilitation is central to long-term freedom from pain. You will receive customized, easy-to-follow daily exercises and ergonomic adjustments tailored to your specific biomechanical profile.
