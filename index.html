Sharp, electric or burning pain radiating down the leg is caused by compressed or inflamed spinal nerve roots. Our multi-modal pathway relieves the compression and calms the irritated nerve.

Slip Disc & Herniation

Migraine & Cervicogenic Headaches

Chronic throbbing migraines, one-sided pounding, and tension headaches often stem from upper cervical spinal subluxations (C1-C2 atlas-axis), suboccipital nerve impingement, and dural tension. Dr. Jayapal Rathod combines cranial osteopathy, gentle chiropractic adjustment, and myofascial release to address the root mechanical cause of migraines.

EXPLORE OSTEOPATHY FOR MIGRAINE →
WHAT TREATMENT INVOLVES
  • C1-C2 Atlas realignment & suboccipital release
  • Craniosacral dural mobilization & venous outflow
  • TMJ (jaw) & temporal muscle trigger point release
  • Autonomic nervous system (Vagus nerve) reset

Gut Health & Lifestyle Diseases (Visceral Osteopathy)

Chronic digestive disorders (IBS, acid reflux, chronic bloating, sluggish digestion) and lifestyle-induced stress are closely linked to the spine via the autonomic nervous system and thoracic diaphragm. Our integrated Visceral Osteopathy, chiropractic neuromodulation, and clinical physiotherapy free organ restrictions and restore balanced motility.
